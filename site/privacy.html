Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy covers Foam for Android, Foam for iOS and Foam for macOS (together, "the app"), made by Snake Pit Software. In short: your notes stay on your device and in the Git repository you choose. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer never receives your notes or your settings.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores is kept on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • Your notes: a copy (clone) of the Git repository you set up, or a local-only repository if you don't connect one.
  • Your settings: the repository URL, branch, username, commit author name and email, and your preferences in the app.
  • Your access token, if you enter one: kept in your system's secure storage (Android's encrypted preferences, the iOS Keychain or the macOS Keychain), and used only to sign in to your Git host.

On Android, the app's data is excluded from system backups. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it from the device.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Your Git host. When the app syncs, it connects over HTTPS to the Git host in your repository URL (for example, GitHub) to download changes and upload yours. It sends your username and access token to sign in, and your notes and commits (which include the commit author name and email you set). What happens to that data there is covered by your Git host's own privacy policy.
  • Images from the web in your notes. If a note includes an image by its web address, the preview downloads that image from the site hosting it, as any web page or Markdown viewer would. That site can see the request, like any other visit, including your IP address.
  • Links you open. Web links in your notes open in your browser, and only when you tap them.

The app sends nothing else. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party services, and the developer receives no data from it.

Permissions

On Android, the app asks only for internet access and to check whether a network connection is available (to sync). On iOS, it uses background app refresh to sync, if your settings allow it.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You control what the app has: you can change or remove your token and settings in the app, delete notes, disconnect the repository, or uninstall the app. Data in your Git repository is managed through your Git host.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
